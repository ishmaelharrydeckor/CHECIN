## 9. Frequently Asked Questions
- **H2:** Questions, answered.
- **Subhead:** The things people ask before they put a tablet on the wall.
**How does a check-in actually work?**
A tablet at your entrance shows a QR code that refreshes every 15 seconds. Employees scan it with their phone. ChecIN records the check-in, and scans again on the way out to record the check-out.
**Can someone check in for a colleague with a screenshot?**
The code changes every 15 seconds, so a photo or screenshot goes stale almost immediately. The tablet is also the only thing that can produce valid codes for your entrance.
**Do employees need to install an app?**
There's nothing to find in an app store. ChecIN opens in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen, so it sits there like any other app icon.
**What hardware do we need?**
One basic tablet at each entrance, mounted and plugged in. Setup takes about ten minutes: you enter a one-time code from your dashboard, and the tablet takes it from there. Adding another office later just means pairing another tablet. If a tablet is ever lost, you switch it off from the dashboard and pair a replacement.
**Is our data kept separate from other companies?**
Yes. Every company's people and records are walled off from every other company's.
